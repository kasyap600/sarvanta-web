Some photographs show the ingredient rather than the milled powder (amla fruit, dried banana, sun-dried tomato, the potato tuber, fresh spinach, dried beetroot, dried lemon, dried mint, carrots, tamarind pulp, dried onion, and ashwagandha root). The cumin-powder photograph shows ground cumin beside whole seeds. The page caption says so. The ashwagandha photograph is of the root and is not a claim about the form supplied.

On 23 October 2026, six photographs were replaced because they showed another company’s pack, a brand, or a watermark: `Cayenne_pepper_1.JPG` (a retail pack labelled “Piment de Cayenne”), `Comino_molido_para_ají_de_fideo.jpg` (Sazón Korr sachets), `Jaggery_powder.png` (a Dhampur Green jar), `Sun_dried_tomato.jpg` (a “Galaxy A12” watermark), `Mint_dried.jpg` (branded tubs in the background), and `Tamarind_paste.jpg` (a Tân Lộc jar). The replacements are also from Wikimedia Commons.
